# Third-party components

Downloaded sources and their upstream licenses are retained locally in `vendor/`, which is not committed. Pinned revisions and recreation instructions are in `docs/build-from-source.md`; the repository retains our patches and the SDK license text. Compiled binaries and extracted game data are not uploaded.

- SCS Telemetry SDK 1.14, SCS Software. Source: https://download.eurotrucksimulator2.com/scs_sdk_1_14.zip . License: `native/SCS-SDK-LICENSE.txt`. Only the read-only position, speed and local-scale channels are used.
- TruckSim Maps, truckermudgeon and contributors. https://github.com/truckermudgeon/maps . Pinned local revision: `d56d0e3fb319230e84284f3029f8bda2c4b572a2`. GPL-3.0-or-later. Full upstream source, license, and submodules remain in `vendor/maps/`. Its parser extracts the user's installed game; it is not loaded in the running overlay.
  - Local compatibility changes: support C-style multiline comments in SII files; `LANE_GUIDE_SKIP_ICONS=1` skips icon decoding; preserve the prefab semaphore's explicit `id` in JSON (it is not its array index). ETS2 1.61 contains a texture format unsupported by the upstream parser. Icon textures, company icons and their POI data are not required for the city-to-city road graph. No road-sector or prefab schema validation was weakened. Patch: `tools/parser-compat.patch`.
  - The local geometry conversion follows the parser's coordinate conventions. This project is offered under GPL-3.0-or-later; see `LICENSE`.
- Electron 44.4.3, Electron contributors. https://www.electronjs.org/ . MIT and bundled third-party notices are in `node_modules/electron/dist/LICENSE` and `LICENSES.chromium.html`.
- ETS2LA plugin, ETS2LA contributors and dariowouters. https://github.com/ETS2LA/plugin . Local revision `7094b334f10b68343d0082f1ef4078669235106b`, ETS2 1.61.x. MIT; full license and source in `vendor/ets2la-plugin/LICENSE.md`. The local DLL build disables the input-override call in `CCore::tick`; patch: `tools/ets2la-read-only.patch`. It also refreshes navigation data on every tick (including cancellation and same-length reroutes), bounds the record count, and publishes an additional GPS output with a timestamp and sequence counter. This patch includes the project's `native/ingame_route_publisher.hpp` and `native/ingame_route_protocol.hpp`; build it within this workspace. The HUD reads `Local\LaneGuideGPSv1` with `FILE_MAP_READ`. Existing status/semaphore readers also remain read-only; no game route or control is changed.
- fmt 11.1.3, fmt contributors. ETS2LA submodule `9ff9c695db8aeadf70eb26498a0e1cdceeeb849a`; license is retained in `vendor/ets2la-plugin/vendor/fmt/LICENSE`.
- Dear ImGui v1.91.9b, Omar Cornut and contributors. https://github.com/ocornut/imgui . Pinned revision `f5befd2d29e66809cd1110a152e375a7f1981f06`. MIT; `vendor/imgui/LICENSE.txt`. The unmodified Win32 and DirectX 11 backends render the native in-game HUD. The installer includes the license alongside the DLL's map data.
- MinHook v1.3.4, Tsuda Kageyu and contributors. https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook . Pinned revision `c3fcafdc10146beb5919319d0683e44e3c30d537`. BSD 2-Clause and bundled HDE notices; `vendor/minhook/LICENSE.txt`. Statically linked for DX11 Present/ResizeBuffers interception. License copied by the installer.
- Malgun Gothic is loaded from the user's Windows Fonts directory at runtime for Korean text. The font is not bundled or redistributed.

Game maps, road templates, and extracted game data remain the property of their respective rights holders. No game data has been uploaded or published by this project.
